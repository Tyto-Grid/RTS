How it works

Three places, one record per house.

1

On the rooftop

Supervisors open a link on their phone – no app store, no password. Photos, serial labels, GPS and forms are captured at the house.

2

In the office

The PM and MIS see each village at a glance: what is complete, what needs a re-visit, what is waiting for a document.

3

On the portal

Files come out in the portal's own format, and the next day's portal reports show what moved.
